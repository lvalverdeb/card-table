## Full description (4000 characters max)
Card Table brings six classic card games to one green felt table: Klondike, FreeCell, Rummy, Hearts, Crazy Eights and Sevens. Everything runs on your device. There are no ads, no accounts, no purchases and no internet connection needed.

SIX GAMES, ONE DECK
• Klondike: the classic solitaire. Build four suits from Ace to King, with Draw 1 or Draw 3, Standard or Vegas scoring, undo and Finish for me.
• FreeCell: every card face up and four free cells to park cards in. Almost every deal can be won.
• Rummy: draw, meld sets and runs, and empty your hand before the computer players. First to 100 points wins.
• Hearts: take tricks, but dodge the hearts and the queen of spades. Low score wins.
• Crazy Eights: match the suit or rank on the pile. Eights are wild and let you call a suit.
• Sevens: lay cards outward from the sevens, suit by suit. First empty hand wins the round.

MADE FOR PLAYING
• Tap a card to send it where it fits, or drag it yourself.
• Undo any move, restart the same deal or deal again.
• Your game is saved as you play and picks up where you left off.
• Five card designs, including a large-print deck.
• Gentle sound effects you can switch off.
• Every game has its own How to play panel.

WORKS ANYWHERE
• Fully offline: nothing is sent or received.
• Available in English, Spanish and French, following your device language.
• Phone and tablet layouts, portrait and landscape.

Made in San José, Costa Rica.
